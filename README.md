## Permit & Approval Guides

### Dubai
- **Dubai Municipality** — Contractor registration, building permits, NOC process: [dm.gov.ae](https://dm.gov.ae)
- **DEWA** — Electrical and water connection approvals for renovation and pool projects
- **Developer NOCs** — Required in Emaar, Nakheel, Meraas, and DAMAC communities before structural work

### Abu Dhabi
- **Abu Dhabi Municipality** — Engineering permits and contractor classification: [adm.gov.ae](https://adm.gov.ae)
- **ADDC** — Utility connection approvals

### Free Zones
- **DIFC, DMCC, Trakhees** — Each has its own fit-out approval process; engage a contractor familiar with the specific authority
